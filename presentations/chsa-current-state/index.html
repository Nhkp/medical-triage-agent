Scope and safety boundary

Assist, never replace

Should support

  • Collect patient-declared symptoms.
  • Structure relevant context.
  • Escalate red-flag signals.
  • Explain priority with an audit ID.

Must not do

  • Make autonomous diagnoses.
  • Prescribe or discharge.
  • Hide uncertainty.
  • Replace clinician judgment.
This slide should be crisp. It is central to stakeholder trust.
