Safety boundary

This is not an autonomous diagnosis system

Allowed

Clinical-staff support, symptom collection, escalation cues, and metadata-only audit.

Forbidden

Final diagnosis, treatment decision, patient-facing medical device claims, and hidden uncertainty.

Make the limitation explicit early. It builds trust.
